# Parts Manual and Diagram Sources

## Research Summary (2026-09-28)

### Sumner.com Terms of Use

Checked: https://sumner.com

**Findings:**
- Sumner.com has a support/manuals section but manuals are not directly linked in simple HTML
- Manuals appear to be behind a form or dynamic loading system
- No explicit terms prohibiting linking to public PDFs
- Standard copyright notices apply to all content

**Approach:** We will link to official Sumner PDFs when found on their public site. We will NOT:
- Rehost or copy Sumner's PDF files
- Extract or republish their diagrams or images
- Reproduce their copyrighted material
