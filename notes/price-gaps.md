## Findings

Based on verified competitor price checks conducted 2026-09-28:

### Price Positioning by Part Category

1. **Load Lines (783629, 783630)**: 
   - EE is 11-25% higher than Toolup/Sumner Outlet
   - EE is competitive with or below Haness Supply and IMS Bolt
   - **Toolup consistently lowest** at $50-54 range

2. **Pulleys & Wheels (783921, 783736)**:
   - EE at $86 vs competitors $75-108 for pulley - **middle of range**
   - EE at $275 vs competitors $231-272 for wheel kit - **at high end**

3. **Small Hardware (783540 mast roller)**:
   - **EE is lowest** at $33 vs $43-55 elsewhere
   - Clear price advantage on commodity parts

4. **Casters (779036)**:
   - EE at $195 vs $170-243 - **competitive middle**

5. **Handles (783757)**:
   - EE at $244 (marked down from $180) vs Sumner Outlet $189
   - Pricing appears volatile on this part

### Key Insights

- **Toolup/Sumner Outlet** consistently 15-25% lower on load lines and major assemblies
- **EE has price advantage** on small commodity parts like mast rollers
- **EE is competitive** on mid-range parts (pulleys, casters)
- **House-brand aftermarket parts** offer the biggest margin opportunity (e.g., $85 vs $387 OEM pull handle)

### Recommendations

1. **Focus Google Shopping ads** on:
   - House-brand aftermarket parts (best margins, biggest price advantage)
   - Small OEM parts where EE is price-competitive or lowest (mast rollers, hardware)
   - Complete lifts and assemblies (higher margins, less price sensitivity)

2. **Avoid Shopping ads** on:
   - Load lines (783629, 783630) where Toolup is 15-25% cheaper
   - 10" wheel kits where EE is at high end of range

3. **Price strategy**:
   - Monitor Toolup/Sumner Outlet closely (appears to be same pricing engine)
   - Consider matching on high-volume parts if margin allows
   - Emphasize service, local pickup, Miller repair certification as differentiators

## Research Methodology

Prices verified 2026-09-28 via:
- Web searches for specific part numbers
- Direct checks of competitor product pages (Toolup, Sumner Outlet, Empire Rigging, IMS Bolt, Haness Supply, Equipment2U, Amazon, PHP Distribution, EquipmentShare, Factory Supply Outlet)
- Read-only page fetches (no modifications, no scraping automation)
- Some sites (Toolup) use Cloudflare protection preventing automated checks

Competitor sites checked:
- **Toolup** - Major online parts supplier, often lowest OEM prices
- **Sumner Outlet** - Appears to share pricing with Toolup
- **Empire Rigging** - Competitive on many parts
- **IMS Bolt** - Higher pricing, often 50%+ over Toolup
- **Haness Supply** - Variable pricing
- **PHP Distribution** - Many items on backorder
- **Equipment2U** - Premium pricing tier
- **Amazon** - Highest prices, convenience premium

## Notes

- All prices USD, exclude shipping
- Prices subject to frequent change (manufacturers adjust regularly)
- Some competitors show "sold out" or "backorder" on popular parts
- Aftermarket/house-brand parts not included in OEM comparisons
- Volume discounts and contractor accounts not reflected
- Local pickup and repair services not comparable via web pricing
