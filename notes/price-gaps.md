## Findings

Based on the traffic analysis report and limited additional research:

1. **Price positioning**: Energized Engines is typically 15-25% higher than Toolup/Sumner Outlet on common OEM parts
2. **Competitive on some items**: Parts like 783540 Mast Roller ($33.00) are below some competitors
3. **House brand advantage**: Aftermarket parts (e.g., EE 2019 pull handle at $85 vs OEM $387) offer significant savings
4. **Recommendation**: Focus Google Shopping ads on house-brand parts and OEM items where EE is price-competitive

## Research Methodology

Price data primarily sourced from the September 28, 2026 traffic analysis report. Additional competitor price checks were attempted but not completed for this preview build (would require systematic scraping of competitor sites, which was beyond scope of read-only demonstration).

To complete this analysis, the owner should:
1. Use Google Shopping price comparison tools
2. Check Merchant Center competitor pricing data
3. Manually verify key competitor prices quarterly
4. Focus on high-volume parts identified in Google Search Console

## Notes

- Prices are subject to change
- Some competitors show "sold out" on certain parts
- Aftermarket/house-brand parts not directly comparable to OEM pricing
- Volume discounts and contractor accounts not reflected in public pricing
